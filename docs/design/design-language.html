Hybrid v3: index-card edges from C

B is the base: the night-map palette, contour rings and soft cards. From A: condensed, heavy column signs, a bold NEXT tag, and the active nav item filled with the accent. From C: the dotted desk under the contours, and done cards struck through. Shown in dark with an orange project accent, and in light with a cobalt one.

Dark, project "Pharmacy POS", accent orange
Light, project "Home renovation", accent cobalt
TypeArchivo condensed at 800 weight for signs only (logo, column headers, section labels, NEXT). Geist for everything you read, and Geist Mono for ids, counts and filter syntax.
ColourGreen-black (or pale survey green in light mode) plus one project accent. Group stripes are the only other colour, and they're always paired with the group's name somewhere.
TextureA dot grid and faint contour rings on the canvas only, never behind text blocks. Both are low contrast and can be switched off in settings.
Shape10px cards and controls, 7px column signs, 4px chips. Group stripe on the left edge of each card. Cards have an ink outline and a hard offset edge, as in C. The next-up card takes the accent edge and sits slightly askew, and done cards lose their edge.
